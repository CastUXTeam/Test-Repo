Understanding the Knowledge Graph

At the heart of the CAST platform lies our proprietary Knowledge Graph - a revolutionary approach to understanding software architecture:

What is the CAST Knowledge Graph?

The Knowledge Graph is a comprehensive, multi-dimensional representation of your entire software landscape. It captures:

  • Code Relationships: How components, classes, and functions interact across your entire codebase
  • Data Flows: How information moves through your application layers
  • Dependencies: External libraries, frameworks, and service integrations
  • Business Context: Connections between technical components and business capabilities
